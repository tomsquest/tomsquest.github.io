Last week I ran an **"IntelliJ Master Championship"** class/presentation. There were prizes to win, like JetBrains yo-yos and IntelliJ stickers.

We had a great time sharing tips and counting points to nominate the "best-intellij-touch-typist-2k17".

The rules were one point per valid answer, and each player answers secretly then the answer is given before I show it on screen and discuss it.
